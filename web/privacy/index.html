1. Personal data we process

  • Account data: email address, account identifier, and authentication-related technical data needed to operate an account. We do not receive or store your password in readable form.

  • Coffee-entry data: the date and time of each coffee entry you choose to save.
  • Technical and security data: limited service, device, browser, and error information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App.
  • Deletion-request data: the email address and any message submitted through the public account-deletion form.

We receive this data directly from you, from your use of the App, and from the providers needed to authenticate and host the service. We do not ask you to provide medical data or use the CAF level as a health measurement.

2. Purposes and legal bases

  • To provide the App and your account: create and manage an account, authenticate you, store and synchronise your entries, and show the requested statistics. Legal basis: performance of a contract or steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)).
  • To secure and maintain the service: prevent abuse, diagnose faults, protect the App, and enforce these Terms. Legal basis: our legitimate interests in operating a safe and reliable service (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)).
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests: where required by applicable law. Legal basis: GDPR Article 6(1)(c).
  • To handle deletion and privacy requests: verify the request, communicate with you, and document compliance. Legal basis: GDPR Articles 6(1)(b), 6(1)(c), and 6(1)(f), as applicable.

3. Recipients and service providers

We use service providers acting on our instructions where necessary to run the App:

  • Supabase for database hosting, authentication, and realtime synchronisation;

If we configure a separate transactional-email provider, it will process the email address and message-delivery data needed to send account confirmation and password-reset messages. The provider will be identified in the relevant email and this Policy will be updated if the change materially affects processing.

5. Retention

Account, profile, and coffee-entry data are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete the account in the App, these data are deleted as part of the deletion operation. If you use the public form, we retain the request and correspondence only for the time needed to verify, process, and document the request, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

6. Is data required?

An email address and authentication data are necessary to create and use an account. If you do not provide them, we cannot provide account-based synchronisation. A display name and coffee entries are optional; without coffee entries, the App has nothing to display in its timer or statistics.
