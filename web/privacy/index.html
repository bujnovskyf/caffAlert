1. Personal data we process

  • Account data: email address, account identifier, and authentication-related technical data needed to operate an account. We do not receive or store your password in readable form.
  • Profile data: an optional display name.
  • Coffee-entry data: the date and time of each coffee entry you choose to save.
  • Technical and security data: limited service, device, browser, and error information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App.
  • Deletion-request data: the email address and any message submitted through the public account-deletion form.

We receive this data directly from you, from your use of the App, and from the providers needed to authenticate and host the service. We do not ask you to provide medical data or use the CAF level as a health measurement.

Optional reminder on Android: if you switch on “Notify me when CAF hits zero” in Settings, the App asks Android for the notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13 and later) and schedules a single local notification on your device for the moment the CAF countdown of your latest entry ends. The reminder is created and stored only by Android on that device; no notification data, push token, or delivery information is sent to us or to any provider, and no push-notification service is used. Your on/off choice is kept only on the device. You can switch it off in the App, or revoke the notification permission in Android settings, at any time; signing out cancels the pending reminder.

Data kept only on your device: to work offline and on the home screen, the App stores a few values locally on your phone or in your browser and does not send them to us beyond the coffee entries above: the time of your latest coffee and the CAF duration for the Android home-screen widget, short-lived markers of recent widget taps (so one tap never logs two coffees), whether the reminder is switched on, your language choice, whether you have finished the introduction, and, in the Android app, a local error log (the file app_logs.txt in the App’s private storage) with the time, message, and technical details of warnings and errors, used only to diagnose problems; the App never uploads this file (error reports sent to Sentry, if enabled, are described in section 3). The error log is not cleared automatically. All of these values are removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data (on the web: clear site data).

3. Recipients and service providers

We use service providers acting on our instructions where necessary to run the App:

  • Supabase for database hosting, authentication, and realtime synchronisation;
  • Netlify for hosting this website and receiving the public account-deletion form;
  • Sentry, only if enabled for a particular deployment, for technical error reporting and a small sample of performance data (for example how long screens or requests take). The Sentry SDK then also generates a random installation identifier so that repeated errors from the same installation can be grouped; it is not linked to your email address. The App is configured not to send default personally identifiable information to Sentry.

If we configure a separate transactional-email provider, it will process the email address and message-delivery data needed to send account confirmation and password-reset messages. The provider will be identified in the relevant email and this Policy will be updated if the change materially affects processing.

5. Retention

Account, profile, and coffee-entry data are kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete the account in the App, these data are deleted as part of the deletion operation. Copies may remain for a short time in Supabase’s automatic backups and service logs until they are overwritten under the provider’s regular retention cycle; they are not used for any other purpose in the meantime. If you use the public form, we retain the request and correspondence only for the time needed to verify, process, and document the request, unless a longer period is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

6. Is data required?

An email address and authentication data are necessary to create and use an account. If you do not provide them, we cannot provide account-based synchronisation. A display name and coffee entries are optional; without coffee entries, the App has nothing to display in its timer or statistics.
