What CaffAlert does

CaffAlert is a simple coffee log. It stores the times of coffee entries added by the user and turns them into overviews and statistics.

CAF level

CAF level is a fictional game indicator. It only counts the time since the last coffee entry. It does not measure caffeine in the body, is not a health measurement, and is not health advice. The app’s copy has an intentionally playful tone.

The user decides whether and how much caffeine to consume. CaffAlert does not replace a doctor or professional medical advice.

Account and data

An email-and-password account is required to sync entries. Users can delete their account directly in the app’s Settings. This permanently removes the sign-in account, profile, and coffee entries.

A request can also be made without access to the app on the account deletion page.
