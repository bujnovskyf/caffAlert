Delete account

Want to delete your CaffAlert account and all related data? If you can access the app, the fastest path is Settings → Delete account.

What is deleted: the sign-in account, profile, and every saved coffee entry. This cannot be undone.

Cannot access the app?

Send the request below using the email address on the account. Before deleting the account, we verify that the request belongs to its owner. We will respond at the email provided.
